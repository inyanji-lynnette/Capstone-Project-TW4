# FAQ & Troubleshooting

Answers to the most common questions and issues reported by QueuEase
users.

## General

### Is QueuEase free to use?

Yes. QueuEase is free for patients. 

Clinics using QueuEase to manage their queues may have their own separate arrangement with QueuEase, but this doesn't affect you as a patient.

### Can I join more than one queue at a time?

Yes. If you have appointments at different clinics on the same day,
each will appear separately under **Active Queues**.

### Does QueuEase work if I lose signal or my phone dies?

Your place in the queue is held on QueuEase's servers, not on your
phone, so a dead battery or lost signal won't remove you from the
queue. However, you won't receive notifications until you're back
online, so it's worth checking your position manually once you
reconnect.

## Troubleshooting

### I didn't receive a notification when my turn was close

1. Check **Settings > Notifications** and confirm **Queue Alerts** is
   turned on.
2. Check your phone's system-level notification permissions for
   QueuEase (**Settings > Apps > QueuEase > Notifications** on most
   phones).
3. If both are enabled and you still aren't receiving alerts, try
   removing and rejoining the queue.

### I joined the wrong queue

Tap the incorrect queue under **Active Queues**, select **Cancel
Appointment**, then join the correct one following the steps in
[Getting Started](/docs/getting-started).

### The app shows my position isn't updating

This is usually a temporary sync delay. Pull down on the queue screen
to manually refresh. If the issue continues for more than a few
minutes, check your internet connection, or restart the app.

### I can't find my clinic when searching

Not all clinics currently use QueuEase. If you can't find yours,
confirm with the clinic directly whether they support QueuEase queues,
or ask at their reception desk for their QR code.
