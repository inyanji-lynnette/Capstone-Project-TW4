# Introduction


**QueuEase** is a mobile app that lets patients check their live position
in a hospital or clinic queue remotely, get notified as their turn
approaches, and reschedule appointments — without needing to sit in a
waiting room for hours just to hold their spot.

This site documents how QueuEase works and how to use it.

## Who this is for

- **Patients** who want to track their place in line from home or work
- **Clinic staff** who want to understand how patients interact with the
  queue on their end
- **Anyone reviewing this project** as a technical writing sample

## Key features

- **Live Queue Tracking** — see your real-time position in line from
  anywhere
- **Smart Notifications** — get alerted as your turn approaches
- **Easy Rescheduling** — change or cancel an appointment in a few taps
