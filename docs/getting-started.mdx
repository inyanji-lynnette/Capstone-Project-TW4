# Getting Started

This guide walks you through installing QueuEase and joining your
first queue, from a completely fresh install.

## Prerequisites

- A smartphone running iOS 15+ or Android 10+
- An active phone number or email address for account verification

## 1. Download the app

Download QueuEase from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) and
install it as you would any other app.

## 2. Create an account

1. Open QueuEase and tap **Sign Up**.
2. Enter your phone number or email address.
3. Enter the verification code sent to you.
4. Add your full name — this is what clinic staff will see when they
   call you forward in the queue.

Your account is now ready to use.

## 3. Join your first queue

1. On the home screen, tap **Join a Queue**.
2. Search for the clinic or hospital you're visiting, or scan the QR
   code posted at their reception desk.
3. Select the service you need (e.g. General Consultation, Lab Results).
4. Tap **Confirm** — you'll be added to the queue and shown your current
   position.

## What happens next

Once you've joined a queue, QueuEase tracks your position automatically.
See [How-To: Track & Reschedule](/docs/how-to-track-reschedule) for what
to do from here.
