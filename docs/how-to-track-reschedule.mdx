# How-To: Track & Reschedule

This guide covers the two most common tasks in QueuEase: checking your
position in line, and changing an appointment you've already booked.

## Check your position in line

1. Open **QueuEase** and tap the queue you've joined under **Active Queues**
   on your home screen.
2. Your current position is shown at the top of the screen, along with
   an estimated wait time.
3. This updates automatically — you don't need to refresh manually.

:::tip
If your estimated wait time changes significantly (for example, if the
clinic runs behind schedule), QueuEase will send you a notification
with the updated estimate.
:::

## Turn on notifications

Notifications are how QueuEase tells you when it's almost your turn, so
it's worth confirming they're enabled.

1. Go to **Settings** > **Notifications**.
2. Make sure **Queue Alerts** is toggled **on**.
3. You'll receive an alert when you're approximately 3 people away from
   being called.

## Reschedule an appointment

1. Go to **Active Queues** and select the appointment you want to change.
2. Tap **Reschedule**.
3. Choose a new available date and time from the list shown.
4. Tap **Confirm New Time**.

Your spot in the original queue is released, and you'll receive a
confirmation for the new time.

## Cancel an appointment

1. Go to **Active Queues** and select the appointment.
2. Tap **Cancel Appointment**.
3. Confirm the cancellation when prompted.

:::caution
Cancelling within 1 hour of your scheduled time may affect your ability
to book same-day appointments at that clinic in the future. Check the
clinic's specific policy if you're unsure.
:::
